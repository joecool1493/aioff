    The sky appears blue because of the scattering of sunlight by the gases and particles in the Earth's atmosphere, a process known as Rayleigh scattering. 135
    Here are the key points that explain why the sky is blue:
    • Wavelength of light. Blue light has a shorter wavelength than other colors, so it is scattered more. 15
    • Sunlight. The sun emits a range of wavelengths, with the bluey-green part being the most prominent. 14
    • Human eye sensitivity. Our eyes are more sensitive to blue light than to violet, which is scattered even more than blue. 124
    Thus, the sky appears blue because blue light is scattered in all directions by the tiny molecules of air in the atmosphere, and the other wavelengths aren't scattered as much. 35
